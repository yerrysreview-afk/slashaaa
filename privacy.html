SLASHA

Privacy Policy

Slasha (“the game”, “we”) is a brush-duel game. This policy explains what information the game handles and the choices you have. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

INFORMATION WE HANDLE

  • Account: if you sign in, your email address and sign-in details (Google, or email and password) are handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see your password.
  • Profile: your player name, nationality, a friend code, and an optional profile icon you choose (resized to a small image on your device).
  • Game data: duels played, wins, losses, rating, performance ranks, and your 40 most recent online duels (opponent name, country, score, result and date).
  • Social data: friend requests and friends, duel invitations, messages you send to friends (up to 300 characters each), players you block, and reports you send.
  • On your device: sound and music settings, your guest profile if you play as a guest, and when you last read a conversation. Guest data never leaves your device.

The game has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell your data.

ONLINE DUELS AND YOUR IP ADDRESS

Online duels connect the two players’ devices directly (peer-to-peer). To set this up, the game uses connection services run by PeerJS and by relay providers such as Metered and Open Relay, plus public STUN servers from Google and Twilio. These services, and your opponent’s device, can see your IP address during a duel. We do not store duel gameplay data.

HOW WE USE IT

To run the game: sign-in, your profile, the leaderboard, friends, messages, challenges and ratings. To keep players safe: reviewing reports and acting on abuse.

WHO CAN SEE WHAT

  • Signed-in players can see your name, flag, icon, rating and win-loss record (on the leaderboard, and when they look you up with your friend code).
  • Messages are visible to you and the friend you send them to. If a player is reported, the report includes that player’s ID and name, your reason and note, and up to the last 10 messages of that conversation. We review reports.
  • Other players’ duel history may keep your display name from the time you played.

SERVICE PROVIDERS

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The game also loads some code and fonts from third-party servers (such as Google and unpkg), which receive your IP address when they load.

KEEPING AND DELETING YOUR DATA

We keep your account data until you delete it. To delete it, open Profile → Delete account (also available in Settings). This permanently removes your account, profile, rating, duel history, friend records, conversations and invitations. If you cannot use the app, email yerrysreview@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it. Copies in backups may remain for a short time. Reports you filed about other players may be kept to protect them and others. Guest data is stored only on your device: delete it from Profile, or by clearing the app’s data.

CHILDREN

Slasha is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

YOUR CHOICES

You can edit your name, nationality and icon in Profile, block or report players, change sound settings, and delete your account at any time. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or to correct it.

SECURITY

Data travels over encrypted connections. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
