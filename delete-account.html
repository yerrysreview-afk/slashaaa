DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT AND DATA

You can delete your Slasha account and the data linked to it at any time.

IN THE APP

  1. Open Slasha and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile icon, scroll to the bottom of your profile and tap Delete account (it is also in Settings).
  3. Type DELETE, confirm with your password or Google, and tap Delete my account.

CAN’T USE THE APP?

Email yerrysreview@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my Slasha account”. We will verify the request and delete your account, normally within 30 days.

WHAT IS DELETED

  • Your account and sign-in
  • Your profile: name, nationality, icon, friend code, rating, record and duel history
  • Your friend records, conversations with friends, and duel invitations

WHAT MAY REMAIN

  • Copies in backups, for a short time
  • Reports you filed about other players, kept to protect them and others
  • Other players’ own duel history may still show your display name from the time you played

Guest data is stored only on your device. Delete it from Profile (Delete saved data) or by clearing the app’s data.
